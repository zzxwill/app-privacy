# Places - Photo Location Finder Support

## About Places

Places is a privacy-first iOS app that indexes your photo library by GPS location, enabling you to browse and search your photos by place. Unlike the standard Photos app, Places provides a clear, searchable list of every city, town, and coordinate found in your library.

## Features

- **Privacy First**: All indexing and reverse geocoding happens entirely on your device. Your data never leaves your phone.
- **Smart Search**: Search by city name, country, or specific coordinates.
- **Interactive Map**: View your photos scattered across a beautiful, high-detail interactive map.
- **Fast Indexing**: Seamlessly scans your library and organizes photos into a searchable hierarchy.
- **Offline Access**: Once indexed, you can browse your locations even without an internet connection.

## How to Use

1. Open Places on your iPhone
2. Grant photo library access when prompted
3. Wait for the initial indexing to complete
4. Browse your photos organized by location in the list view
5. Use the search bar to find specific cities or countries
6. Switch to the map tab to see your photos on an interactive map

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does the app need access to my photo library?

Places reads GPS metadata from your photos to organize them by location. It only reads location coordinates — it does not modify, upload, or share your photos in any way.

### Does the app upload my photos or data?

No. All data processing happens entirely on your device. No images, metadata, or personal information are sent to any external server.

### Why are some photos not showing up?

Photos without GPS metadata (location data) cannot be mapped to a location. Photos taken with location services disabled or imported without location data will not appear in Places.

### How long does indexing take?

The initial indexing time depends on the size of your photo library. For most libraries, it completes within a few minutes. Subsequent launches are much faster as only new photos need to be indexed.

### What iOS version is required?

Places requires iOS 17.0 or later.

## Troubleshooting

### Photos not loading

1. Ensure you have granted photo library access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Places
2. Try restarting the app
3. If the issue persists, try restarting your iPhone

### Map not displaying

1. Ensure you have an internet connection for the initial map tile loading
2. Check that location services are enabled if you want the map to center on your current position
