# Privacy Policy for Places - Photo Location Finder

**Last updated: April 3, 2026**

## Overview

Places is an iOS app that indexes your photo library by GPS location, enabling you to browse and search your photos by place on an interactive map.

## Data Collection

Places does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information.

- No personal data is collected
- No usage analytics
- No tracking
- No cookies
- No third-party advertising services
- No data transmitted to external servers

All data processing happens entirely on your device.

## Data Access

The app requests access to your Photo Library solely to read GPS metadata (latitude and longitude) from your photos. No images or metadata are sent to any external server.

The app may also request access to your device location to center the map on your current position.

## Data Storage

Photo location data is indexed into a local SQLite database stored on your device. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

Places uses Apple's built-in CLGeocoder service for reverse geocoding (converting GPS coordinates to place names). This is an Apple system service and is subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).

## Children's Privacy

Places does not collect any information from children or any users.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
