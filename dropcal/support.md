# DropCal Support

## About DropCal

DropCal is an iOS/iPadOS/macOS app that intelligently converts text and images into calendar events using on-device AI. It also syncs events from Apple Calendar to Google Calendar.

## Features

- **Smart Event Parsing**: Paste text, upload images, or take photos to automatically extract event details
- **On-Device AI**: Uses Apple's Vision framework and Foundation Models — no cloud processing
- **Apple Calendar Integration**: Save parsed events directly to your Apple Calendar
- **Google Calendar Sync**: Sync events from Apple Calendar to Google Calendar
- **Duplicate Prevention**: Tracks synced events to avoid duplicates
- **Background Sync**: Optional automatic syncing in the background

## How to Use

### Creating Events from Text or Images

1. Open DropCal
2. Paste text, pick an image, or take a photo containing event details
3. The app will automatically extract event information (title, date, time, location, description)
4. Review the parsed event in the preview
5. Tap to save to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar

### Syncing to Google Calendar

1. Sign in with your Google account
2. Select events to sync, or enable automatic sync
3. Events from your Apple Calendar will be synced to Google Calendar

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does DropCal send my data to the cloud?

No. All text and image processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning. No data is sent to external servers for processing.

### What platforms are supported?

DropCal requires iOS 17.0+ or macOS 14.0+.

### Why does DropCal need camera access?

Camera access allows you to take a photo of a flyer, poster, or invitation and have the app automatically extract event details from the image.

### Do I need a Google account?

Only if you want to use the Google Calendar sync feature. DropCal works with Apple Calendar without a Google account.

### Why are some events not parsing correctly?

On-device AI works best with clearly formatted text and legible images. Try ensuring the text is well-structured or the image is clear and well-lit.

## Troubleshooting

### Google Sign-In not working

1. Ensure you have a stable internet connection
2. Try signing out and signing back in
3. Check that your Google account allows third-party app access

### Events not appearing in calendar

1. Ensure DropCal has calendar access in Settings > Privacy > Calendars
2. Check that the correct calendar is selected
3. Try restarting the app
