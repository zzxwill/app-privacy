# Privacy Policy for DropCal

**Last updated: April 5, 2026**

## Overview

DropCal is an iOS/iPadOS/macOS app that converts text and images into calendar events using on-device AI, and syncs Apple Calendar events to Google Calendar.

## Data Collection

DropCal does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

- No personal data is collected by us
- No usage analytics
- No tracking
- No cookies
- No third-party analytics services

## On-Device Processing

All text and image processing is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning (Vision framework and Foundation Models). Your text, photos, and images are **never** sent to any external server for processing.

## Calendar Access

DropCal requests access to your Apple Calendar (via EventKit) to create and read events. This data stays on your device and is managed by Apple's system frameworks.

## Google Calendar Integration

If you choose to use the Google Calendar sync feature, DropCal will:

- Authenticate via Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0)
- Access your Google Calendar to create and manage synced events

This connection is made directly between your device and Google's services. We do not have access to your Google account credentials or calendar data. Google's use of your data is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Camera and Photo Library

DropCal may request access to your camera and photo library to capture or select images for event extraction. These images are processed on-device only and are not uploaded or stored beyond the immediate processing session.

## Data Storage

Event sync records are stored locally on your device to prevent duplicate syncing. This data never leaves your device.

## Children's Privacy

DropCal does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
