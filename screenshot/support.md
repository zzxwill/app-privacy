# Screenshot — Quick Copy Support

Screenshot is a Mac menu bar utility that copies a selected area or window to your clipboard. The Mac App Store version requires macOS 14 or later.

## Capture and paste

1. Open Screenshot and click **Capture Selection**, or choose **Capture Selection & Copy** from the camera icon in your menu bar.
2. Drag an area on one display. To capture a window instead, press Space and click the window. Press Escape to cancel.
3. Paste into another app with Command-V. In Preview, use **File → New from Clipboard**.

The default capture shortcut is Command-Shift-A. To change it, click the menu bar camera icon and choose **Set Hotkey…**. If a shortcut is occupied, choose a different combination containing Command, Option, or Control.

## Screen Recording permission

macOS requires Screen Recording permission to capture screen content. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording** (called **Screen & System Audio Recording** on some versions), enable Screenshot, and relaunch the app if prompted. Screenshot captures still images only; it does not record audio.

## Where are screenshots saved?

Screenshot places the image on the macOS clipboard; it does not create an image file or upload it. Use the app you paste into to save a file. macOS clipboard features and other apps may handle clipboard content according to their own settings.

## Finding the app

Screenshot runs in the menu bar without a Dock icon. Click the camera icon to capture, set a shortcut, open the help window, or quit.
