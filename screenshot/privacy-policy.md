# Screenshot — Quick Copy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Screenshot is a macOS menu bar utility developed by Zhengxi Zhou.

## Screen content and clipboard

With your macOS Screen Recording permission, Screenshot uses Apple's ScreenCaptureKit to obtain available displays and windows and capture the area or window you select. The selected image is processed on your Mac and placed on the macOS clipboard. Screenshot does not upload screen content, save a screenshot history, or record audio.

Other apps can use clipboard content, and macOS features such as Universal Clipboard may transfer clipboard content according to your system settings. These functions are controlled by macOS and the other apps, not by Screenshot.

## Local preferences

Screenshot stores your chosen keyboard shortcut and whether you have seen the welcome window in its local app preferences. It does not transmit these preferences.

## Data collection

Screenshot includes no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, or network requests. The developer does not collect personal information through the app.

## Permission controls

You can revoke Screenshot's screen access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording. Screen capture will stop working until access is restored.

## Support

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in your email is used to respond to your request. Avoid including private screen content.

Questions about privacy: **support@ai-infra.org**.
